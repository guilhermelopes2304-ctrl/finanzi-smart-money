# FINANZZI Roadmap

## Dark-first redesign
Orange #FF5A1F + black #111111, premium fintech, Banco Inter-inspired clarity and Pierre-inspired editorial/product rhythm.

## Motion and perceived performance
Skeletons, lazy loading where appropriate, purposeful entry/exit/state/loading/progress motion, reduced-motion support, and no gratuitous continuous effects.

## Observability
OpenTelemetry plus a compatible error/performance backend, with privacy-safe telemetry.

## Quality
Biome/Commitlint/Knip/Arch-contract/Stryker-Stryke evaluation, plus unit/integration/Playwright E2E and Codecov.
