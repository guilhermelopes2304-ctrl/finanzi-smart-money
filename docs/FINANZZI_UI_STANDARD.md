# FINANZZI UI Standard

## Motion

Use https://github.com/kylezantos/design-principles as the motion reference when accessible. Motion should communicate hierarchy, causality, feedback, or state. Prefer transform/opacity, 200–400ms transitions, purposeful ease-out/ease-in-out timing, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Loading

Every asynchronous interface must expose a suitable skeleton, spinner, progress indicator, or explicit loading state. Lazy-load non-critical images/content where useful. Cover entry, exit, loading, success, error, empty, disabled, responsive, and progress states when applicable.

## Product direction

Official FINANZZI identity: orange #FF5A1F and black #111111, with white and dark neutrals. Visual direction is premium fintech, combining Banco Inter-inspired clarity and product focus with Pierre-inspired editorial/product rhythm, without copying either product.
